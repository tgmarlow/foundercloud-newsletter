The people, ideas, and connections founders are building right now.

If you're a founder who still personally answers every customer request, this one is for you. It's been a minute since the last issue, so I'll get right to it.

Here's the pattern I keep seeing. Say a six-person team gets 40 customer requests in a week, and the founder tries to land all of them at once. Something crashes, and it's usually the roadmap. The same thing happens with AI agents. Everyone spins one up, nobody owns it, and a month later you have nine agents and no idea which ones matter. The fix is boring. Put the requests in order, give every agent a named owner, and spend more of your week conducting than playing.

Two posts below. Air Traffic Control covers sequencing customer requests without wrecking the plan, and The Dispatch Desk covers who owns the agents once everyone needs them. We've also started sharing one-page versions on Instagram. Start with whichever problem is on your desk this week.
