The people, ideas, and connections founders are building right now.

If you're a founder making decisions before the numbers are clear, this one is for you. Say you have 22 trials, three customer conversations that disagree with each other, and a call to make by Friday. That's normal at this stage.

Sailors had a method for this. Dead reckoning estimates where you are from your last reliable fix, your course, your speed and the time elapsed. The estimate drifts, so you sail a short leg and check it against a fresh fix. The same habit helps when you hand work to AI agents overnight. Write down what they can do alone, what waits for your review in the morning, and what never leaves your hands.

Dead Reckoning covers making good calls on thin data, and The Night Shift covers what to hand your agents while you sleep. There's also a one-page version of The Night Shift on Instagram at @foundercloud. Start with whichever one matches the decision on your desk this week.
