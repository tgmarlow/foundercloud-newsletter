The Build Sheet from FounderCloud

The people, ideas, and connections founders are building right now.

Founders, the proof that matters this early is one person who does the job using your product on a bad day. Say your demo wins over three investors on Monday. By Thursday, the billing manager at your first real customer is back in her spreadsheet because of a split claim nobody showed you.

This week's two posts come at that from different ends. The Local Guide is about getting a domain insider into the loop before you call a demo ready. Its Three Messy Cases test has that person walk through three ugly, real workflows while you write down every hesitation. Base Camp is about counting the people who would actually miss you. Its Would You Miss It? test tells you more than a launch-day signup count ever will.

Both tests fit on one page, and the worksheets are in the posts if you want to screenshot them. Pick one and run it before Friday. If you need an introduction to someone who does the job, ask in the FounderCloud community.
